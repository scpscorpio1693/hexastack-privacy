Hexa Stack – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game Hexa Stack ("the Game", "we", "us") handles information.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store personal information such as your name, email address or contacts. Your game progress (level, coins, settings) is stored only on your device.

2. Advertising

The Game shows ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID
  • IP address and approximate (coarse) location derived from it
  • Device and app information, plus ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

How Google uses this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, we ask for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy Choices in the Game. You can also reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android device settings.

3. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never receive your payment details. Google Play gives us a purchase confirmation so we can deliver what you bought.

4. Children

The Game is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Data retention and deletion

Game progress stays on your device. Uninstalling the Game or clearing its app data deletes it. Data collected by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's policies.

6. Security

All network communication by the advertising and billing SDKs uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).

7. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
